# OpenNexus Community Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

OpenNexus welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds.

OpenNexus 欢迎来自不同背景的开发者和用户友善参与。

## Expected / 我们鼓励

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal attributes.
- Give specific, constructive, and evidence-based feedback.
- Protect user privacy, business data, and security details.
- Respect maintainers' time and the project's stated support boundaries.
- 聚焦问题与代码，提供具体、建设性的反馈。
- 尊重隐私、业务数据、安全边界和维护者时间。

## Unacceptable / 不可接受

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberate humiliation
- Publishing personal information, credentials, or private business data
- Repeated spam, malicious submissions, or attempts to bypass security controls
- 骚扰、歧视、威胁、人身攻击、泄露个人或业务敏感信息，以及恶意提交

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.

为保护社区和项目，维护者有权编辑、隐藏或删除不当内容，并限制相关账号参与。
